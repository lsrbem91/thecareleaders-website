Consultancy

Employment and pathways

Care leavers are more likely than their peers to be out of education, employment or training, and the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 widens every council’s corporate parenting duties. We help councils act like a family business for their care leavers, with real jobs at the end of it.

Start a conversation See the work

Is this for you?

Services usually come to us when:

Your care leavers aren’t getting into work, and your apprenticeships aren’t reaching them.

You need to show how you’re meeting wider corporate parenting duties.

Local employers want to help but don’t know where to start.

Your local offer is written for inspectors, not for care leavers.

What we do

The Family Business

The council acting like a family business for its care leavers, with apprenticeships, employers and a Skills Academy. Examples: Suffolk, Kirklees.

Employer partnerships and events

Opening doors with employers. Examples: a partnership workshop with PwC, and employer engagement for Oxfordshire County Council.

Leaving care local offers

What care leavers are entitled to, written so they can use it. Example: West Sussex, with an accessible online platform.

How commissioning works

Most commissions follow the same five steps.

Step 3BriefNamed outcomes, a timeline and a price, agreed before work starts.

Questions commissioners ask

How is The Family Business different from an apprenticeship scheme?

An apprenticeship scheme offers places. The Family Business changes how the whole council behaves: services ring-fence roles, managers are ready to support care leavers, and local employers join in.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the work. We agree a price in the brief before anything starts, so there are no surprises. Contact us for a price.

How long does it take?

Most work runs for three to 12 months. We set the timeline with you in the brief.

Who do you involve?

Your leaders and staff, your partners, and the children, young people and families who use the service. We agree who’s involved, and how, in the brief.

How do we buy from you?

Smaller pieces are usually a direct award or a quote. Larger pieces may go to tender, and we’re happy to talk before you write the specification.
