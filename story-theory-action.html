The problem it solves

Services want in-house trainers and care-experienced facilitators to run their own sessions, but people are often handed slides rather than a method. Sessions then lean on theory or on personal story alone, and staff leave with nothing to use the next day.

Helps withBuilding in-house champions and trainers

What changes

  • Open a session with a story chosen for a clear purpose.
  • Link each story to the theory that explains it, in minutes.
  • Close every session with a tool or activity people can use.
  • Handle timing, energy and difficult moments with more confidence.
  • Are supported by staff trained with real examples, not just slides.
  • Hear real stories shared safely and with purpose.
  • Meet staff who leave training with something practical to try.
  • Choosing and telling a story with purpose and care
  • Pairing a story with the right theory
  • Designing activities that turn learning into action
  • Running a room: timing, energy and difficult moments
  • Sharing lived experience safely as a facilitator

Questions commissioners ask

Do people need training experience already?

No, but they should expect to train colleagues or young people afterwards. Groups are kept to 12 so everyone designs and runs a session, whether they are new to it or experienced.

Is it only for care-experienced facilitators?

No. It suits in-house trainers, participation workers, designated teachers and practice leads. Care-experienced facilitators get specific support on sharing their lived experience safely, alongside the professional experience they bring.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
