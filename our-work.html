Durham County CouncilWe trained a peer researcher and made a documentary and seven short films on how Durham cares.2025 to 2026

Durham County Council: How Durham Cares

For Durham’s Families First reforms, we trained a peer researcher to interview staff, young people and families about how Durham shows it cares. The result was a 30-minute documentary and seven short films, launched to staff in March 2026.

35+practitioners and young people interviewed
15 hoursof interviews recorded over six filming days
7short films, alongside a 30-minute documentary
Client
Durham County Council, Children and Young People’s Services
Who took part
Over 35 practitioners and young people, across more than 25 services
Our role
Design, peer researcher coaching, production and project management, with filmmaker Sam Hamper
Norfolk County CouncilWe are building a scheme where care-experienced adults are trained and coached to mentor children in care.2026 to 2027

Norfolk Virtual School: mentors who have been there

Children in care asked for mentors who understand their journeys. We are building a peer mentoring scheme with Norfolk Virtual School, where care-experienced adults are trained, coached and supported to mentor children in care.

Client
Norfolk Virtual School, Norfolk County Council
Who took part
Care-experienced adults and the virtual school team
Our role
Programme design, mentor training and coaching model

Liverpool Virtual School: Rising Futures

Delegates at Liverpool’s 2024 conference asked to hear from young people directly, not just about them. For 2025 the Virtual School asked us to help produce Rising Futures, and young people designed and ran part of the day themselves.

50people gave feedback on the 2024 conference, which shaped the brief
5young people designed and ran their own workshop
12 monthsof The Care Leaders Online for every delegate
Client
Liverpool City Council, Liverpool Virtual School
Who took part
Headteachers, designated teachers, social workers, partners, and young people supported by the Virtual School
Our role
Conference production, branding and bookings, hosting, and the Tools for Schools workshop
Kent County CouncilWe trained around 490 school and social care staff through six sessions that followed one child’s journey through care.2022 to 2023

Virtual School Kent: A Tool for Change

Every session followed Sam, a child whose story ran from before birth into care. By the end, around 490 staff across Kent’s schools and services could see Sam in the children in front of them.

490staff signed up across three cohorts (174, 145 and 171)
6twilight sessions per cohort
124 of 125said the first session met its objectives
Client
Virtual School Kent, Kent County Council
Who took part
Teachers, school staff, college and social care staff
Our role
Six-session training programme, keynote, event management
Oxfordshire YouthWe helped a youth work charity design, pitch for and win a five-year supported accommodation contract.2020 to 2021

Oxfordshire Youth: Young People’s Supported Accommodation

Oxfordshire Youth wanted to bring a youth work approach to supported accommodation for young people aged 18 to 24. We helped the charity shape its proposal and its pitch, then plan how to move 130 young people into the new service.

5 yearslength of the supported accommodation contract Oxfordshire Youth is part of
130young people a year the service reaches, in Oxfordshire Youth’s words
18 to 24age range of the young people the service supports
Client
Oxfordshire Youth
Who took part
Oxfordshire Youth’s leadership and service team
Our role
Service design, pitch coaching and a trauma-informed plan for setting up the service
Rotherham Metropolitan Borough CouncilWe looked at 11 children’s services through young people’s eyes, and a new participation model followed.2017

Rotherham MBC: services through young people’s eyes

In 2017 we asked young people, care leavers and the professionals around them how Rotherham’s children’s services looked from where they stood. The scope covered 11 service areas, and within months the council had moved and strengthened its participation service.

11service areas scoped, from the Virtual School to independent reviewing officers
3 tiersin the participation model agreed afterwards: ages 4 to 11, 11 to 15, and 15 and over
5 monthsfrom the scope to our follow-up review of what had changed
Client
Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council, Children’s Services
Who took part
Young inspectors, the Looked After Children’s Council, care leavers, foster carers and professionals
Our role
Led by our founder before The Care Leaders was set up: scoping conversations, report and follow-up review
Stockport CouncilWe trained foster carers and social workers to write and read the referrals and profiles that arrive before a child does.2016

Stockport Council: Referral and profile training

In 2016 we designed and ran training for Stockport’s fostering service on the referrals and profiles that arrive before a child does: how they are written, how they are read, and what they mean for the child.

2one-day courses: one for social workers, one for foster carers
5Fostering National Minimum Standards the training was mapped to
1toolkit for foster carers and social workers, delivered with the August 2016 training
Client
Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council, Fostering and Family Placement
Who took part
Foster carers, social workers and senior managers
Our role
Led by our founder before The Care Leaders was set up: design, training pack and delivery, with trainer Karylle Phillips
City of York CouncilWe brought young people in care into the co-design of Making York Home.2015 to 2016

City of York Council: Making York Home

York wanted every child in its care to be fostered in York. As part of Innovation Unit’s team, we brought young people in care into the design of Making York Home, through the co-design workshops and design sprints.

9days of youth engagement and co-design, December 2015 to June 2016
1strategy for involving young people in the research and design
4young people signed up to the design sprints, alongside 24 professionals
Client
City of York Council, with Innovation Unit
Who took part
Young people in care, foster carers, social workers and council staff
Our role
Led by our founder before The Care Leaders was set up: youth engagement and co-design within Innovation Unit’s team
