Thank you for your enquiry

Thanks for thinking of Luke for your event.

Your enquiry has reached us. Luke checks his diary for your date and you’ll get an email with availability, the fee and the keynote we’d recommend for your audience. In the meantime, here’s a short message from Luke about what every booking includes.

Luke’s welcome video is coming soon.

More than a keynote

Every booking, in person or online, comes with the same package, so the learning doesn’t stop when the session ends.

The keynote

Story-led, practical and built around your audience. Luke combines his lived experience of care with years of leading change in children’s services, from care leaver to care leader.

The recording, for 12 months

Every delegate can watch the full keynote again on The Care Leaders Online for 12 months after your event, and share it with colleagues who couldn’t be in the room.

A workbook for every delegate

Built around Story, Theory and Action, with activities and materials that help delegates take the learning back into their teams.
