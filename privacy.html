Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

This notice covers thecareleaders.com. The website is run by The Care Leaders Ltd (company number 10782615), registered office Office 207, Unit 5, 399-405 Oxford Street, London W1C 2BU. We are the data controller for the personal information collected here. You can contact us about your data at hello@thecareleaders.com.

What we collect

Keynote booking form: your name, role, organisation, email, a mobile number for the day of the event, and the details of your event.

Emails and calls: whatever you choose to tell us when you contact us.

Cookies: this website doesn’t set cookies or use analytics. To show our fonts, your browser loads them from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address.

We don’t ask for sensitive information, and we ask you not to include personal details about children or families when you contact us.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and arrange a booking: our lawful basis is legitimate interests, because you’ve asked us to get in touch.

To deliver a booking you’ve confirmed: our lawful basis is contract.

We only send you marketing if you’ve chosen to receive it, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted services to run the website, our forms and our email: GitHub (website hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and Microsoft 365 (email and forms). Some case studies include films hosted on YouTube, shown in privacy-enhanced mode; YouTube only receives information about you if you play a film. If your enquiry is about a keynote, we share it with Luke Speaks Ltd, which contracts and invoices keynotes. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for two years after our last contact with you. If you book with us, we keep booking and invoice records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@thecareleaders.com and we’ll respond within one month.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
