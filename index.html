Who we are

A children’s social care consultancy that brings lived experience of care and professional leadership into the same room.

Since 2017 we’ve delivered 40 projects for 36 local authorities and national bodies, including the Department for Education. We work alongside children, care leavers and the staff who support them, so the people a service is designed for help shape the decisions about it, and every recommendation is backed by evidence.

From care leaver to care leader is the idea we were founded on. In 2026 that approach won Gold for Lived Experience at the iESE Public Sector Transformation Awards.

Not lived experience as a guest speaker slot. Lived experience as strategy.

Department for Education: Fostering National Minimum Standards

We led the national engagement on the rewrite of England’s fostering standards, with councils, independent fostering agencies and partner organisations across England.

Durham County Council: NVR in residential care

120 residential staff across 11 children’s homes trained in non-violent resistance.

Regional Care Cooperative, South East: Youth Participation Collective

Young people from across the South East designed a Shadow Board of 18 Youth Ambassadors to give children in care a say in regional decisions.

Suffolk and Kirklees: The Family Business

Councils acting like a family business for their care leavers, with ring-fenced apprenticeships, local employers and a Skills Academy. Built in Suffolk, now being set up in Kirklees.
