Lawyers Who Care: helping a new legal mentoring organisation get off the ground

Lawyers Who Care matches care-experienced people who want a career in law with legal mentors. In 2024 we supported its founders from idea to registered CIC: advice on legal form and funding, help with the articles of association, a website and introductions.

The starting point

Lawyers Who Care started from a gap its founders saw: no legal mentoring organisation existed for care-experienced people. By February 2024 they had a pitch deck and a plan, but big questions were still open: should it be a CIC, a membership model or a charity, where would seed funding come from, and who would do the admin?

What we heard

The founders told us what they needed: help choosing a legal form, a plan for seed funding, and capacity for the admin that comes with starting an organisation. We worked from their own business plan and pitch deck rather than writing new ones, and kept the decisions with them.

What we did

Strategy At a strategy meeting on 23 February 2024, Luke worked through the founders’ plan and pitch deck with them. Model We advised on the membership model, including a pilot offer instead of founding members. Register Our Chief of Staff helped complete the articles of association. Companies House confirmed the CIC by 26 March 2024. Launch We built the LWC website, coached the team on cash flow and their roadmap, and introduced them to Virtual School Heads, funders and other partners.

How we worked

Their plan, not ours

The founders wrote the business plan and pitch deck. We coached, challenged and filled gaps.

Practical help

Where advice wasn’t enough, we did the work: the articles of association and the website.

Opening doors

We introduced LWC to people who could fund it, host it and refer to it.

Luke’s own route, from experience of care to leading The Care Leaders and The Care Leaders Fellowship, is why we back care-experienced people and their allies when they start something new.

What changed

Lawyers Who Care launched on 17 May 2024 at Garden Court Chambers in London, and is still running. By May 2026 it had matched 126 care-experienced aspiring lawyers with legal mentors, and it runs legal careers workshops and seminars with law firms.
