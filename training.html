The Care Leaders Learning

Training your staff will still be talking about a year later

The Care Leaders Learning brings all our training together. Face-to-face courses for your teams, and online learning people can take when it suits them. Every course is built the same way: a real story, the theory behind it, and tools people use the next day.

Trauma-informed training that pairs lived experience of care with professional experience from the field, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about trainingSee the online platform

Why train with us

There are plenty of trauma-informed trainers. Very few have lived the care system and led it.

Lived and professional experience, in every room

People who grew up in care and people who have led children’s services design and deliver every course together. Not a guest speaker: the whole course.

Story, theory, action

Every session opens with a real story, adds the evidence behind it and ends with a tool people use the next day.

Built inside real services

Our courses were made with councils, schools and children’s homes: NVR in 11 Durham homes, and around 490 school staff trained in Kent.

Close to national policy

Luke Rodgers BEM led engagement on the DfE’s rewrite of the national fostering standards. We have worked with 36 local authorities and national bodies.

Built for your service

Every course is shaped around your team, your procedures and your children. If we do not have it, we build it.

Recognised for it

Children & Young People Now Workforce Development Award 2023, and iESE Gold for lived experience in 2026.

How we teach

Every course and keynote follows the same spine.

1Story

Lived experience opens every session, alongside professional experience from the field. It makes the subject real and gets people listening.

2Theory

The evidence underneath gives the learning rigour.

3Action

Practical tools mean people leave able to do something differently the next day.

For whole teams and organisations

Most of our learning is bought by organisations for their people: virtual schools for their schools, fostering services for their carers, councils for their workforce. Teams of five or more can learn together on the platform, and whole organisations can take a licence. Ask us what fits.
