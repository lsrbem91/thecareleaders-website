A full development day for whole-school staff teams on raising aspirations for children in care and with a social worker. Five sessions that move from what holds children back to what each adult in a school can do about it.

The problem it solves

Children in care often meet low expectations at school, even when nobody means it. Over time those expectations shape what they believe about themselves and what they aim for. Without a whole-staff plan, it falls to the designated teacher alone.

Helps withSchool attendance and attainment

What changes

  • Notice and challenge low expectations in how they talk about children
  • Spot a child’s strengths and build on them in class
  • Talk about the future in ways that stretch without setting children up to fail
  • Own their part of the school’s aspiration plan
  • Teachers who expect more of them, and help them get there
  • Classrooms and corridors that tell them they belong
  • Being known for what they are good at
  • Honest, hopeful conversations about what comes next
  • How low expectations quietly shape a child’s path
  • How environments shape what children believe about themselves
  • Spotting and growing a child’s strengths

Questions commissioners ask

Can our designated teacher lead parts of the day?

Yes. The day is designed so designated teachers can lead it with us, which helps the plan keep going after we leave.

Does it fit an INSET day?

Yes. It runs as one day in five sessions for a whole-school staff team, with a participant workbook and a school action plan to follow up afterwards.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
