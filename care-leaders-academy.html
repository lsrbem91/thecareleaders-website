Programme

The Care Leaders Academy

Turning care experience into leadership. The Academy brings together care-experienced young people aged 13 to 18 and gives them the skills to help their council: speaking in public, improving services and co-designing what comes next.

Ask about the Academy

At a glance

Who it’s forCare-experienced young people aged 13 to 18
Who commissions itCouncils and virtual schools
TrainingOne or two days, shaped around each cohort
After the trainingLive tasks inside the council

How it works

  1. 1
    Skills audit

    We start with your council: what you need young people’s help with, and what they want to learn.

  2. 2
    Build the programme

    Sessions shaped around the cohort and the live tasks ahead.

  3. 3
    Recruit

    A six-week digital marketing toolkit to reach young people in your area.

  4. 4
    Train

    One or two days of leadership training, adapted to the group.

  5. 5
    Lead

    Young people take on real tasks: a keynote, input to staff training, or a co-design project.

What young people do

  • Speak in public about their experience
  • Help improve services
  • Co-design new work alongside staff
