Fostering

Councils need more foster carers and need to keep the ones they have, and the new national standards are asking services to change the way they provide fostering. We led the national engagement on the rewrite of those standards for the Department for Education. With Why Care, we help councils recruit, assess and support foster carers, and put the new standards into practice.

Start a conversation See the work

A partnership with Why Care

Separate companies with complementary skills, both led by care-experienced leaders.

The Care Leaders brings

Transformation expertise: strategy, service design and making change stick. Leadership of the national engagement on the new fostering standards.

Why Care brings

Fostering expertise: recruiting, assessing and supporting foster carers, and the Fostering Diagnostic. Lead drafting of the new fostering standards.

For your information: Luke Rodgers is Chief Executive of The Care Leaders and co-CEO of Why Care. All fostering work is contracted through Why Care.

Is this for you?

Services usually come to us when:

You’re losing foster carers faster than you can recruit them.

Your region is setting up a fostering hub and needs to plan how it will meet the new standards.

You want an honest outside view of your fostering service before inspection.

Your foster carers and the children they care for don’t feel heard in how the service runs.

Questions commissioners ask

Why have The Care Leaders and Why Care partnered?

Because improving fostering takes two sets of skills at once. The new standards ask services to change how they work, which is transformation. Doing it well means finding, assessing and keeping the right carers, which is fostering. We bring the first and Why Care brings the second, so neither company pretends to do the other’s job.

Are they the same company?

No. They’re separate companies. Luke Rodgers leads The Care Leaders and is co-CEO of Why Care, so we tell you that upfront. All fostering work is contracted through Why Care.

What do the two companies have in common?

Both are led by care-experienced leaders, and both start from the same belief: services get better fastest when the people who use them help shape them.

Have you worked together before?

Yes, on the new national standards. Nikita Samuels-Gillars of Why Care was lead drafter and Luke Rodgers of The Care Leaders led the national engagement with councils, independent fostering agencies and partner organisations.

Who will we work with day to day?

Luke Rodgers leads for The Care Leaders and Nikita Samuels-Gillars for Why Care. Why Care holds the contract.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the work. We agree a price in the brief before anything starts, so there are no surprises. Contact us for a price.

How long does it take?

Most work runs for three to 12 months. We set the timeline with you in the brief.

Who do you involve?

Your leaders and staff, your partners, and the children, young people and families who use the service. We agree who’s involved, and how, in the brief.

How do we buy from you?

Smaller pieces are usually a direct award or a quote. Larger pieces may go to tender, and we’re happy to talk before you write the specification.
