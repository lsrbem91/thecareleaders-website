Programme

Care to Work

An employment programme for care-experienced young people. Two strands run side by side: work experience at 16 to 18, and paid roles at 18 to 25. Each follows the same steps, with personal advisers, mentors and managers trained to support young people at work.

Ask about Care to WorkSee where it started

At a glance

Who it’s forCare-experienced young people aged 16 to 25
Strand 1Work experience with employers across your area
Strand 2Paid roles, starting inside your council
How it’s boughtOne strand or both, with a shared set-up

Two matching pathways

Both strands follow the same nine steps in the same order, so they can run side by side.

What your council keeps

  • A tested route for creating roles and placements for care leavers
  • The Mentors Toolkit and the Managers Toolkit
  • Trained mentors, managers and personal advisers
  • An impact report and a handover your team can run again
