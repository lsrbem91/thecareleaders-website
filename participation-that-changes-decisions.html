Voice

Voice is only worth it if it changes something.

Most services say children’s voices shape their work. Fewer can point to a decision that changed. This course is for the staff who run participation, and the managers who decide what happens next, so young people see the difference they make.

The problem it solves

Most services run consultations, councils and surveys, then struggle to name a decision that changed because of them. Young people notice when nothing comes back and stop turning up. Boards and inspectors want to know what difference children’s views made, not how many sessions ran.

Helps withChildren’s voice and participationGetting ready for inspection

What changes

  • Plan participation around a decision someone has agreed to make.
  • Use methods young people want to come back for.
  • Take young people’s views to the managers who decide, by an agreed route.
  • Tell young people what changed, what did not, and why.
  • See what changed because they spoke up.
  • Get an honest answer when something cannot change.
  • Are kept safe and paid fairly when they take part.

Questions commissioners ask

Why do managers need to come, not only participation staff?

Because the gap is usually between the session and the decision. Managers hear what young people need from them, and both groups leave with the same plan for getting views to decisions and answers back.

Does it cover children in care councils?

Yes. Children in care council leads are part of the audience. The course looks at how council members’ views reach corporate parents and services, and how members hear what happened next.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
