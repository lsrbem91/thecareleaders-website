Relationships and NVR

Tools you can use tomorrow morning.

A hands-on course built from our practice tools, including PACE, Spot Soothe Shift, the Window of Tolerance and co-regulation. Each tool is taught the same way: the story behind it, the theory under it, and a chance to practise it.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about this courseSee all training

At a glance

  • LengthHalf day to full day. Tools also on demand, one at a time
  • FormatFace to face, live online or on demand
  • Who it is forResidential and fostering teams
  • Group sizeUp to 30, so everyone practises
  • IncludesA workbook for each tool covered
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Residential and fostering teams
  • School staff
  • Family Help lead practitioners and early help workers, with a version built for the new Family Help teams
  • Supervisors who want a shared language for their team

What they learn

  • PACE: playfulness, acceptance, curiosity and empathy in real conversations
  • Spot Soothe Shift: noticing distress and helping a child back to calm
  • The Window of Tolerance, and how to widen it
  • Co-regulation before reasoning
  • Choosing the right tool for the moment

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1Pick your tools

We agree with you which tools your team needs most

2Story

Where each tool comes from in a real child’s life

3Theory

Why it works, in a few minutes, not an hour

4Practice

Role play, scenarios and feedback

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

On demand

Self-paced on The Care Leaders Online, with a CPD certificate, for individuals or a whole team.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
