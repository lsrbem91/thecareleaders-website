Espresso

Short, strong training for busy teams. Six 15-minute video sessions that give anyone working with children a bite-size way into understanding children in care, children in need and families who have a social worker. Each one ends with three things to try.

Each session pairs lived experience of care with the professional experience of teachers and social workers who support children every day.

Ask about Espresso

At a glance

Who it’s forAnyone working with children: schools, early years, health, youth work and community teams
FormatSix video sessions of about 15 minutes
Each sessionOne idea, what it looks like day to day, three things to try
TakeawaysA one-page sheet with a prompt to discuss as a team

The six sessions

One child’s story runs through all six. First built with East Sussex Virtual School for its schools.

1Born to love and be loved
2ACEs: beyond the buzzword
3Attachment, not attention
4Trauma and transitions
5Education meets care
6Language that lifts
