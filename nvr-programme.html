Programme

NVR across your service

Non-violent resistance, built into how your whole team works. We start with what your staff need, then build a programme around them and around their rotas. Staff learn in cohorts, with work to do between sessions, so the approach is part of daily practice by the end.

Ask about the programmeSee it in Durham

At a glance

Who it’s forChildren’s homes, fostering services and early help teams
Built aroundYour staff’s needs and their shift patterns
FormatCohorts, with online modules and work between sessions
A way inNVR Essentials, our one-day course

How it works

  1. 1
    Start with your staff

    We look at what your teams need and how their shifts run.

  2. 2
    Build the programme

    Sessions, online modules and materials shaped around your service.

  3. 3
    Learn in cohorts

    Staff train in groups, at times that keep safe staffing.

  4. 4
    Between sessions

    Online modules and practice tasks to try at work.

  5. 5
    Make it yours

    The approach sits inside your own practice framework, with materials your team keeps.

Where it works

1In children’s homes

Less restraint and stronger relationships. Example: 120 staff across 11 children’s homes in Durham.

2With foster carers

Helping carers stay calm, keep their presence and build support around the child, so homes hold.

3In early help and family work

Equipping practitioners to coach parents facing aggressive or controlling behaviour at home.
