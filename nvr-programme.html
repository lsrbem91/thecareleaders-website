The Care Leaders’ non-violent resistance programme

The NVR Programme Calm adults. Stronger relationships. NVR that stays.

Non-violent resistance for children’s homes, fostering and family support services. Every member of staff learns the approach through NVR Essentials, up to 12 of your leaders become champions through NVR Advanced, and a 90-day plan keeps it going after we leave.

The NVR Programme, in short

What we offer, what we do, what you get and what it is for.

The offer

NVR for your whole service in three stages: NVR Essentials for every member of staff, NVR Advanced for up to 12 champions, and a 90-day plan to embed it.

What we do

We health-check your practice against the seven pillars, train every member of staff, coach your champions over a term and support them as they run their first sessions.

What you get

The NVR Toolkit, a trainer pack your champions deliver from, coaching and debrief routines, and a 90-day plan for every champion.

What it achieves

Adults who stay calm and present when behaviour escalates, fewer restrictive interventions, and NVR your own people can teach to new staff.

Our mission

Presence, not pressure.

When a young person’s behaviour escalates, most staff are taught what to stop doing. NVR gives them something to do instead: stay calm, stay present, resist the behaviour without fighting the child, and repair the relationship afterwards. The NVR Programme makes that the way your whole service works, then leaves your own people leading it.

Every adult trained.Champions in every home.NVR that stays.

What the NVR Programme sets out to do

Three objectives, for every service we work with.

Practice

Every member of staff using the same seven pillars, so a young person meets the same response whoever is on shift.

Leadership

Up to 12 champions who coach colleagues through the hardest moments and train new staff themselves.

Legacy

A 90-day plan, an agreed way to show impact and a trainer pack, so NVR keeps going after we leave.

1Online, 90 minutes

3Online, 90 minutes

Questions commissioners ask

Who is it for?

Children’s homes, fostering and family support services that want NVR to become how the whole service responds when behaviour escalates, not a one-off training day.

Can we buy NVR Essentials on its own?

Yes. NVR Essentials runs as a standalone course for any team. The NVR Programme adds the champions stage and the 90-day plan, so the approach lasts.

Who should be a champion?

Team leaders, deputy and registered managers, practice leads and experienced carers who other staff already turn to, and who will be in post for the year ahead.

How many champions?

Up to 12 in a cohort, so every champion gets coached. Larger services can run a second cohort.

Do champions need NVR Essentials first?

It helps, and in the full programme they do it with their teams. Champions who missed it start with the NVR Toolkit before the first block.

What happens after the programme?

Each champion has a 90-day plan, runs a session for their own team with us observing the first one, and joins a network with champions from other services. You keep the trainer pack and the NVR Toolkit.

Where does NVR come from?

Non-violent resistance was developed by Haim Omer to help parents respond to aggression without escalating it. We have adapted it for children’s homes, fostering and family support, and every session pairs it with lived experience of care.

How is it priced?

Every service is different, so we quote for your numbers of staff and champions. Tell us about your service and we’ll come back to you within 3 working days.
