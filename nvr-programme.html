NVR and relationships

Staff who use NVR become staff who lead it.

Three online sessions, three face-to-face days and the NVR Toolkit to keep. Up to 12 of your team leaders and managers learn to coach colleagues through the seven pillars of non-violent resistance and to train their own teams, so NVR stays in your service after we leave.

Length
About a term
How
Face to face or live online
Group

For

The problem it solves

Most NVR training stops when the course ends. Practice drifts when staff change or a hard week comes along, and services buy the same training in every year. You need your own people coaching colleagues and training new staff.

Helps withBehaviour, incidents and restraintBuilding in-house champions and trainers

What changes

  • Champions coach colleagues through the hot moment, resistance and announcements
  • Champions run debriefs after hard moments using shared routines and templates
  • Champions deliver NVR training to their own teams from a trainer pack
  • Managers can show the impact of NVR in their service
  • The same NVR approach from adults, even when staff change
  • Adults coached to stay present through the hardest moments
  • Adults who return to the relationship after conflict, because reconciliation is practised
  • How to health-check your service’s NVR practice against the seven pillars
  • Working every pillar on a real case from your own service
  • Coaching colleagues through de-escalation, resistance, announcements and sit-ins
  • Supporter roles, self-care and debrief routines
  • How to plan, rehearse and deliver an NVR session for your own team
  • Evidencing impact, and a 90-day plan to keep NVR going
  • Team leaders and deputy managers
  • Registered managers
  • Practice leads
  • Experienced carers who will champion NVR in their service

Session by session

  • Story
  • Theory
  • Action

Every part opens with a real story, adds the evidence behind it and ends with something people use the next day. Open a part to see what happens.

1Where your service is now

Online, 90 minutes. Meet the cohort. Champions health-check their service against the seven pillars, choose one young person or case to work on throughout, and get onto the NVR Toolkit.

You leave with A seven-pillar health-check of your service and a chosen case

2Presence and de-escalation

Face to face, 10am to 3:30pm. Parental presence, de-escalation and baskets on real situations from your homes. Champions practise cold-iron lines and the hot-moment protocol.

You leave with A plan for what to try with your team before block two

3Review and plan

Online, 90 minutes. What happened when champions used cold-iron lines and baskets with their team. Then reconciliation and acts of resistance, and the work to do before the next day.

You leave with Reconciliation and resistance to try before the next day

4Resistance, reconciliation and coaching

Face to face, 10am to 3:30pm. The pillars staff find hardest to hold under pressure, and how to coach colleagues through them. Supporter roles, self-care, and debrief practice in small groups.

You leave with Coaching and debrief routines, practised in small groups

5Evidencing impact

Online, 90 minutes. Champions bring their announcements and their evidence. We agree how each service will show impact, and start planning the first session champions will run for their own teams.

You leave with An agreed way to show impact in your service

6Announcements, sit-ins and facilitation

Face to face, 10am to 3:30pm. Announcements and sit-ins, then facilitation skills. Champions rehearse the session they will deliver to their team and agree their 90-day plan.

You leave with A rehearsed team session, the trainer pack and a 90-day plan

Questions commissioners ask

How is this different from NVR Essentials?

NVR Essentials is one day that gives a team the framework. NVR Advanced runs over about a term for up to 12 people, and trains them to coach colleagues and deliver NVR to their own teams, so the approach stays in your service.

What support do champions get after NVR Advanced?

A 90-day plan each, a network with champions from other services, and observed co-delivery of their first team session. They keep the NVR Toolkit.

Who is it for?

Team leaders, deputy and registered managers, practice leads and experienced carers who will champion NVR in their service.

Do they need NVR training first?

It helps. Champions who have done our one-day NVR Essentials, or equivalent foundation training, start further ahead. The first block covers the foundations through the Toolkit for anyone who hasn’t.

How many people per cohort?

Up to 12 champions, so everyone gets coached.

How is it spread out?

Three blocks over about a term. Each block opens with a 90-minute online session and ends with a face-to-face day, with work to do in between.

What is the NVR Toolkit?

Our online NVR course on The Care Leaders Online: foundations, parental presence and the seven pillars, each with a short video, reading and an activity to download. Champions keep access after the programme.

What do champions do between sessions?

They try the tools with their team and on their chosen case, and bring back what happened. Each online session starts from that.

Where does it work?

Children’s homes, fostering services and early help teams.

What do we need to provide?

A venue for the three face-to-face days, the delegate list, and cover so champions can attend. We give you the delegate materials and a short brief for managers.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
