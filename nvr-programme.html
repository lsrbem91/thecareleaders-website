The NVR Programme

Staff who use NVR become staff who lead it.

Three online sessions, three face-to-face days and the NVR Toolkit to keep. Up to 12 of your team leaders and managers learn to coach colleagues through the seven pillars of non-violent resistance and to train their own teams, so NVR stays in your service after we leave.

Our NVR trainers combine professional experience of leading children’s homes and fostering practice with lived experience of being a child those services were for.

The NVR Programme, in short

What we offer, what we do, what you get and what it is for.

The offer

A programme over about a term for up to 12 champions from your service: three online sessions, three face-to-face days, work between sessions and the NVR Toolkit online.

What we do

We health-check your NVR practice, work through the seven pillars on your real situations, coach your champions through the hardest moments, and train them to deliver NVR to their own teams.

What you get

Trained NVR champions, a trainer pack, the NVR Toolkit for every champion to keep, a 90-day plan each and a network after the programme.

What it achieves

NVR that lasts inside your service, led by your own people, so you aren’t buying the same training in every year.

Our mission

Delegates arrive as practitioners and leave as champions.

Most NVR training stops when the course ends. Practice drifts when staff change or a hard week comes along. The NVR Programme runs over a term, not a day, and trains a small group of your own people to coach colleagues through the hard moments and to deliver NVR themselves, so the approach stays in your service.

What the programme sets out to do

Three objectives, for every champion on the programme.

Practice

Every champion works through the seven pillars on a real case from their own service, between sessions, with the Toolkit.

Coaching

Champions learn to coach colleagues through de-escalation, resistance, announcements and sit-ins.

Leadership

Champions deliver NVR training to their own teams, with a trainer pack, a 90-day plan and a network behind them.

How it works

From health-check to champions leading NVR in your service, over about a term.

  1. 1

    Health-check

    In the first online session, champions audit their service against the seven pillars and choose a case to work on.

    • Where NVR is strong and where it slips
    • A real case per champion
  2. 2

    Work the pillars

    Across three blocks, every pillar is taught face to face and practised in between with the Toolkit.

    • Tools tried in your homes, not just in the room
    • What happened, brought back each time
  3. 3

    Learn to coach

    Champions coach colleagues through the hardest moments: the hot moment, resistance, announcements.

    • Coaching and debrief routines
    • Practice in small groups
  4. 4

    Learn to facilitate

    Champions plan and rehearse the session they will run for their own team.

    • A trainer pack to deliver from
    • Observed co-delivery afterwards
  5. 5

    Keep it going

    A 90-day plan per champion and a network across services.

    • NVR led from inside your service
    • The Toolkit to keep

The six sessions

Three blocks. Each block opens with a 90-minute online session and ends with a face-to-face day. Between sessions, champions try the tools with their team and on their chosen case, and the next online session starts from what happened.

Questions commissioners ask

Who is it for?

Team leaders, deputy and registered managers, practice leads and experienced carers who will champion NVR in their service.

Do they need NVR training first?

It helps. Champions who have done our one-day NVR Essentials, or equivalent foundation training, start further ahead. The first block covers the foundations through the Toolkit for anyone who hasn’t.

How many people per cohort?

Up to 12 champions, so everyone gets coached.

How is it spread out?

Three blocks over about a term. Each block opens with a 90-minute online session and ends with a face-to-face day, with work to do in between.

What is the NVR Toolkit?

Our online NVR course on The Care Leaders Online: foundations, parental presence and the seven pillars, each with a short video, reading and an activity to download. Champions keep access after the programme.

What do champions do between sessions?

They try the tools with their team and on their chosen case, and bring back what happened. Each online session starts from that.

Where does it work?

Children’s homes, fostering services and early help teams.

What do we need to provide?

A venue for the three face-to-face days, the delegate list, and cover so champions can attend. We give you the delegate materials and a short brief for managers.
