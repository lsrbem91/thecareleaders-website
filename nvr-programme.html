The NVR Programme

Staff who use NVR become staff who lead it.

Three days of training plus our online NVR course. Team leaders and managers learn to coach the hardest parts of non-violent resistance and to train their own teams, so NVR keeps going after we leave.

Our NVR trainers combine professional experience of leading residential and fostering practice with lived experience of being a child those services were for.

The NVR Programme, in short

What we offer, what we do, what you get and what it is for.

The offer

Three days of training plus online learning, for up to 12 team leaders and managers who already know the basics of non-violent resistance (NVR).

What we do

We health-check your NVR practice, coach your champions through the hardest parts of NVR, and train them to deliver it to their own teams.

What you get

Trained NVR champions, a trainer pack, a champion toolkit, online course access, a 90-day plan and a network after the programme.

What it achieves

NVR that lasts inside your service, led by your own people, so you aren’t buying the same training in every year.

Our mission

Delegates arrive as practitioners and leave as champions.

Most NVR training stops when the course ends. Practice drifts when staff change or a hard week comes along. The NVR Programme trains a small group of your own people to coach colleagues through the hard moments and to deliver NVR training themselves, so the approach stays in your service.

Revisit.Coach.Send off.

What the programme sets out to do

Three objectives, for every champion on the programme.

Coach

Champions coach colleagues through the hardest parts of NVR, including the hot moment.

Facilitate

Champions deliver NVR training to their own teams, with a ready-made trainer pack.

Lead

Champions lead NVR in their service, with a 90-day plan and a network behind them.

Explore the programme

Choose a day to see what happens and what champions leave with.

Day 1

Revisit and co-design

We start from where your service is. Champions health-check their practice against the seven pillars of NVR, bring the real situations they are facing, and start coaching others through them.

On the day

  • A seven-pillar health-check of your practice
  • Working on your team’s real challenges
  • Coaching colleagues through announcements

By the end

  • A shared picture of where NVR is strong and where it slips
  • Your own cases ready to work on
Day 2

Coaching the hardest elements

The parts of NVR staff find hardest to hold under pressure, and how to coach colleagues through them.

On the day

  • Strike while the iron is cold: lines that hold
  • The hot-moment protocol
  • The sit-in
  • Coaching skills and debriefs

By the end

  • Confidence to coach the hard moments
  • A debrief routine your team can use
Day 3

Facilitate and send-off

Champions learn to deliver NVR training to their own teams, and leave ready to do it.

On the day

  • Facilitation skills
  • Managing the room
  • Practising with the trainer pack

By the end

  • A first session planned for your team
  • A 90-day plan to keep NVR going
Online

Online learning

Champions get access to our NVR Essentials course on The Care Leaders Online, so they can refresh the basics and share them with their teams.

What it covers

  • The NVR stance and the seven pillars
  • De-escalation and delayed responses
  • Reconciliation gestures

How it works

  • Self-paced, on The Care Leaders Online
  • Learners can work through it at their own pace
After

After the programme

We don’t disappear after day three. Champions join a network and we observe them co-delivering, so the training they run lands well.

What happens

  • A post-programme network for champions
  • Observed co-delivery with your champions

What you have

  • Champions running NVR training in-house
  • The trainer pack and champion toolkit to keep

What your service gets

By the end, NVR is led from inside your service.

  • Up to 12 NVR champions trained to coach and to train
  • A trainer pack: facilitator guide, slides, workbook and activities
  • A champion toolkit: coaching prompts, debrief templates and announcement templates
  • A 90-day plan for each champion
  • Access to our NVR Essentials course on The Care Leaders Online
  • A post-programme network for your champions
  • Observed co-delivery of your champions’ first training
  • NVR training capacity inside your service, so you don’t buy the same training in again

Questions commissioners ask

Who should attend?

Team leaders, deputy and registered managers, and practice leads who will champion NVR in their service.

Do they need NVR training first?

Yes. Champions need NVR foundation training or our one-day NVR Essentials before they start.

How many people per cohort?

Up to 12.

Where does it work?

Children’s homes, fostering services and early help teams.

What happens after the three days?

Champions join a post-programme network, and we observe them co-delivering their first training.

We are new to NVR. Where do we start?

With NVR Essentials, our one-day taster.
