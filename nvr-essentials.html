Course: NVR Essentials

One day. A full framework.

A one-day introduction to non-violent resistance for residential and fostering teams. No prior NVR knowledge needed. Your staff leave with the framework, a pack to run a session with their own team, and 12 months’ access to the NVR Toolkit online.

Trained by people with professional experience of leading NVR in residential and fostering services, and lived experience of the children they are for.

  • FormatFace to face, at your venue
  • Who it is forResidential managers, fostering team leaders, commissioners and practitioners

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Residential managers and staff in children’s homes
  • Fostering team leaders, supervising social workers and foster carers
  • Early help and family workers coaching parents
  • Commissioners deciding whether NVR is right for their service

What they learn

  • What NVR is and why it works
  • The seven pillars, and the three dimensions of presence
  • De-escalation: cold-iron lines, baskets and the hot-moment protocol
  • Reconciliation gestures and acts of resistance
  • How to run a first NVR session with their own team

How it is delivered

Choose the format that fits your staff and your diary.

1

Face to face

One day at your venue, for up to 20 people.

2

As the start of The NVR Programme

NVR Essentials as the foundation, then The NVR Programme to train the champions who keep it going.

What you get, and what you do

What you get

  • Slide decks to introduce NVR to your team
  • A step-by-step facilitator guide
  • Scripts, templates, reconciliation gesture menus and reflection tools
  • 12 months’ access to the NVR Toolkit online for every participant
  • CPD certificates for participants

What you and your staff do

  • Attend the day and bring real situations from your setting
  • Plan your first session back at work before you leave
  • Work through the NVR Toolkit online afterwards
  • Run your first team session within a month

What changes

By the end of the course.

  • Staff share one framework and one language for responding to violence, aggression and coercion
  • The hot moment is handled with a protocol, not instinct
  • Reconciliation becomes routine rather than forgotten
  • Every participant can run an NVR session with their own team
  • A clear next step into The NVR Programme for the people who will lead it

Questions people ask

Do we need any NVR knowledge first?

No. NVR Essentials starts from the beginning.

How many people can attend?

Up to 20, so everyone practises.

What is the NVR Toolkit?

Our online NVR course on The Care Leaders Online: foundations, parental presence and the seven pillars, with a video, reading and an activity for each. Participants get 12 months’ access.

What happens after the day?

Participants run a first session with their own team. Those who will lead NVR in your service can go on to The NVR Programme.

Can it be online?

The day works best face to face. Ask us about a live online version for a dispersed team.
