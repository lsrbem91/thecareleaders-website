One-day taster

NVR Essentials

One day. A full framework. Everything you need to start leading non-violent resistance in your setting. No prior NVR knowledge needed.

Trained by people with professional experience of leading NVR in residential and fostering services, and lived experience of the children they are for.

Ask about NVR EssentialsSee NVR in Durham

At a glance

Who it’s forResidential managers, fostering team leaders, commissioners and practitioners
How longOne full day
Group sizeUp to 20 people
After the day12 months’ access to the online NVR course

How the day works

Morning: understanding NVR

  • The NVR stance and why it matters
  • The seven pillars in depth
  • De-escalation: short lines, baskets and delayed responses
  • The evidence, and how NVR fits Ofsted expectations

Afternoon: applying and leading it

  • Reconciliation gestures and acts of resistance
  • Real residential and fostering scenarios
  • How to run a session with your own team
  • Planning your first session back at work

What you take home

  • Slide decks to introduce NVR to your team
  • A step-by-step facilitator guide
  • Scripts, templates, reconciliation gesture menus and reflection tools
  • 12 months’ access to the online NVR course

After NVR Essentials

Once your team has done NVR Essentials, they can go on to The NVR Programme: three online sessions and three face-to-face days that turn practitioners into NVR champions.
