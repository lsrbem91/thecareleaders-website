Course: The Journey Through Care

One child’s story, the whole care journey.

Our flagship course follows Sam from before he came into care to the day he left. Six sessions of 60 minutes, each pairing his story with the theory behind it, so school staff, social care teams and carers understand what life is like for children in care and what to do differently.

Told through lived experience of the care journey, and taught with professional experience of the schools and social work teams around it.

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Teachers, teaching assistants and designated teachers
  • Social workers, personal advisers and early help staff
  • Foster carers and residential staff
  • New starters in children’s services, and anyone who wants to understand the journey through care

What they learn

  • What happens to a child before, during and after care, from the child’s side
  • Child development, adverse childhood experiences, attachment, trauma and loss
  • How to read behaviour as communication and respond to it
  • The language and recording that shape a child’s life, and how to get them right
  • What school, social care and leaving care look like to the child, and how to work across them

The six sessions

Each session is 60 minutes and follows the next part of Sam’s story. Choose a session to see what it covers.

Session 1

Before care

What happened before a child came into care, and what it does to a developing brain and body. Sam’s story starts before birth.

Covers

  • Child development
  • Adverse childhood experiences
  • Parenting styles and what children learn from them
Session 2

Entering care

Why children really enter care, what the different types of care are, and what the first days and weeks feel like from the child’s side.

Covers

  • The reasons children enter care
  • Types of care: foster, residential, kinship
  • What a placement move feels like
Session 3

Attachment, trauma and loss

Attachment theory and trauma, told through what Sam did and didn’t do with the adults around him.

Covers

  • Attachment and attachment styles
  • Trauma and loss
  • Relationship-focused practice
Session 4

Behaviour as communication

Emotional regulation, the window of tolerance, and what behaviour is telling you when a child can’t say it.

Covers

  • Emotional regulation
  • The messages in behaviour
  • What to do in the moment
Session 5

Language and recording

The words adults use about children in care, in meetings and in records, and what it is like to read your own file years later.

Covers

  • Language that cares
  • The impact of recording
  • Writing with the child in mind
Session 6

School, social care and leaving care

Education for children in care, where school and social care clash, and what leaving care really means.

Covers

  • Education for children in care
  • Where school and social care meet
  • Leaving care

How it is delivered

Choose the format that fits your staff and your diary.

1

Live online

Six 60-minute sessions, usually one a week after school, facilitated live for your cohort.

2

Face to face

The same six sessions as twilight sessions, or compressed into a development day for a whole staff team.

3

Licensed recordings

The series recorded for your organisation, licensed for a year on your own platform or ours, so every new starter gets the same course.

What you get, and what you do

What you get

  • Six live sessions, or the licensed recordings
  • Handouts for every session and the slides to keep
  • A reflection task between sessions
  • CPD certificates for participants who complete the course
  • A short impact report at the end of a cohort

What you and your staff do

  • Attend six sessions of 60 minutes, or work through the recordings
  • Do a short reflection task between sessions
  • Bring a real child, anonymised, to think about across the course
  • For whole-school cohorts: a named lead who gathers the staff and the feedback

What changes

By the end of the course.

  • Staff can see the care journey from the child’s side, and see it in the children in front of them
  • Behaviour is read as communication rather than defiance
  • Language in meetings and records changes
  • Schools and social care staff understand each other’s world
  • In Kent, 124 of 125 said the first session met its objectives and 36 of 36 said the last one did

Questions people ask

How long is each session?

60 minutes. Six sessions make the full course, usually one a week.

Is it online or in person?

Both. Live online is the most common. Face to face works as twilight sessions or a development day.

Can we have the recordings?

Yes. We record the series for your organisation and license it for a year, on your platform or ours.

Who delivers it?

Facilitators with lived experience of care and professional experience of the schools and social work teams around it. Sam’s story is told from lived experience.

How many people can join?

Whole-service cohorts work. Kent ran three cohorts of 145 to 174 staff. Licensed recordings have no limit.

Is it CPD certified?

Participants who complete the course receive a CPD certificate from us.
