The Journey Through Care

Our flagship training follows one child’s journey through care, from what happened before to leaving care. Each session pairs the story with the theory behind it, so staff understand what life is like for children in care and how to support them.

Told through lived experience of the care journey, and taught with professional experience of the schools and social work teams around it.
