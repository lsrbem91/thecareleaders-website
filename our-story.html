Our story

From care leaver to care leader.

At 19, Luke Rodgers BEM gave his first keynote at a National Fostering Agency event. He has spent his career since then inside children’s services, bringing his lived experience of care and his professional experience into the same room. This is the story of how that became The Care Leaders.

Choose a year, or use the arrows, to move along the road.

2010Finding a voice

The first keynote

At 19, Luke was asked to speak to children in care at a National Fostering Agency event about fruit and veg. A young person asked him to talk about care instead.

The NFA’s chief executive asked him to say it again at their national conference.

1 of 15

2012Finding a voice

Ambassador, and a first company

Young Person’s Ambassador for the National Fostering Agency, and his first company, running workshops on resilience and empathy.

He started teaching social work students, and Children & Young People Now profiled him as a “role model of resilience”.

2 of 15

2013Finding a voice

A Children & Young People Now award

An achievement award at the Children & Young People Now Awards, and international work with the British Council in Serbia.

3 of 15

2014Finding a voice

A first social enterprise

Backed by UnLtd, his first social enterprise built youth participation with children’s services and reached 1,000 children in care.

He won UnLtd’s Young Social Entrepreneur Award, the judges’ choice, and joined the Rees Centre advisory board at the University of Oxford.

4 of 15

2015Finding a voice

Global Fellow, national award

Named a Laureate Global Fellow by the International Youth Foundation, and won Entrepreneur of Excellence at the National Diversity Awards.

He brought young people in care into the design of Making York Home.

Making York Home

2017Building The Care Leaders

The Care Leaders is founded

Luke founded The Care Leaders: lived experience of care and professional experience in the same room.

Early work included seeing Rotherham’s services through young people’s eyes.

Rotherham
