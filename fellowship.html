The Fellowship

The Care Leaders Fellowship

A 12-month entrepreneurial leadership programme for anyone with an idea, project or business that solves problems for care-experienced people. Your idea doesn’t need to be polished.

At a glance

Who can applyAnyone over 18 who is care-experienced or works with care-experienced people
How long12 months, from March 2027
TimeAbout four hours a month, plus two retreats
ApplicationsOpen in November 2026
