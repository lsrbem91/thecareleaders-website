The problem it solves

Councils must now co-produce their care leaver local offer with care leavers and show what every department offers. An offer written without care leavers is hard to find and harder to trust. This course brings staff and care leavers together to build an offer care leavers recognise, use and trust.

Helps withCare leavers, work and the local offerChildren’s voice and participation

What changes

  • Involve care leavers as paid partners, with support to take part safely.
  • Bring housing, jobs, health and leisure into the offer.
  • Write entitlements so care leavers can find and understand them.
  • Review the offer every year and tell care leavers what changed.

Questions commissioners ask

Should care leavers attend the training?

Yes. The course is designed for council staff and care leavers in the same room. We help you plan how care leavers are invited, paid and supported, so taking part is safe and worth their time.

We already have a local offer. Is this still useful?

Yes. We start from the offer you have, test it against the guidance and with care leavers, then plan the changes and a yearly review.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
