Voice

Young people leading the change.

A short, lively leadership course for young people in care and care leavers: confidence, speaking up, working with adults in power and leading a project of their own. The Care Leaders Academy is the full programme; Power to Lead is the place to start.

One day, or a series of sessions
How

Group

The problem it solves

Children in care councils and youth ambassador groups are asked to speak up in rooms full of adults, often with little preparation. Without support, the same few voices carry the load, and young people can end up sharing more of their story than they meant to.

Helps withChildren’s voice and participation

What changes

  • Support young people to lead a project, not just attend meetings.
  • Prepare young people properly before they meet senior adults.
  • Respect a young person’s choice to keep parts of their story private.
  • Follow up each young person’s leadership plan after the course.
  • Know their strengths and talk about them with confidence.
  • Choose what to share about their story, and what to keep private.
  • Understand how decisions are made in a council, and by whom.
  • Lead a small project of their own, with a plan to do it.

Session by session

  • Story
  • Theory
  • Action

Every part opens with a real story, adds the evidence behind it and ends with something people use the next day. Open a part to see what happens.

1Me

Young people look at their strengths and their story, and decide how much of it they want to share, and when.

You leave with A clearer sense of their strengths and what they will share.

2Us

Working as a team, leading others and speaking up in rooms full of adults, with practice in a safe space first.

You leave with Practised ways to speak up in meetings with adults.

3Them

How councils and services really make decisions, who holds the power, and where young people can have influence.

You leave with A map of who makes decisions and how to reach them.

4Next

Each young person plans a small project they will lead, with the steps and support they need.

You leave with A personal leadership plan for each young person.

Questions commissioners ask

How does this fit with The Care Leaders Academy?

Power to Lead is the place to start. The Care Leaders Academy is the full programme for young people aged 13 to 18, over several weeks with live tasks. Power to Lead can stand alone or lead into it.

What ages is it for?

Young people aged 13 to 25 who are in care or have left care. We shape the examples and activities around the ages in the group, and keep it to 16 so everyone takes part.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
