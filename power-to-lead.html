Home / Training / Power to Lead
Voice and lived experience

Young people leading the change.

A short, lively leadership course for young people in care and care leavers: confidence, speaking up, working with adults in power and leading a project of their own. The Care Leaders Academy is the full programme; Power to Lead is the place to start.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about this courseSee all training

At a glance

  • LengthOne day, or a series of sessions
  • FormatFace to face
  • Who it is forChildren in care councils
  • Group sizeUp to 16 young people
  • IncludesA personal leadership plan for each young person
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Children in care councils
  • Young ambassadors and participation groups
  • Care leavers forums
  • Young people aged 13 to 25

What they learn

  • Telling your story your way, and when to keep it private
  • Speaking up in rooms full of adults
  • How decisions are really made in a council
  • Planning and leading a small project
  • Working as a team

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1Me

Strengths, story and confidence

2Us

Leading a team and working with adults

3Them

How councils and services make decisions

4Next

A project each young person will lead

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
