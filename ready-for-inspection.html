Voice

Can you show inspectors what children say?

Inspection is moving towards hearing children’s lived experience directly. This course helps children’s home managers and leaders capture what children say, act on it and show the difference it makes, every day rather than the week before an inspection.

The problem it solves

Inspection is moving towards hearing children’s lived experience directly. Homes that gather views only before a visit struggle to show what children said and what changed. This course helps managers build everyday listening, and records that show impact in children’s own words.

Helps withGetting ready for inspectionChildren’s voice and participation

Questions commissioners ask

Is this only for children’s home managers?

It is written for children’s homes, their managers and responsible individuals. Heads of service, quality leads and Regulation 44 visitors are part of the audience too, because they review the same evidence.

Will this help children perform well for inspectors?

No, and it should not. The course is about hearing children every day so inspectors see the real picture. We cover how to prepare children for a visit without coaching what they say.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
