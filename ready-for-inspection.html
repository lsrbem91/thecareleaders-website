Voice and lived experience

Can you show inspectors what children say?

Inspection is moving towards hearing children’s lived experience directly. This course helps children’s home managers and leaders capture what children say, act on it and show the difference it makes, every day rather than the week before an inspection.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about this courseSee all training

At a glance

  • LengthHalf day
  • FormatFace to face or live online
  • Who it is forRegistered managers and deputies
  • Group sizeUp to 24 managers and senior staff
  • IncludesA voice and evidence toolkit for your home
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Registered managers and deputies
  • Responsible individuals
  • Heads of service and quality leads
  • Independent visitors and Regulation 44 visitors

What they learn

  • What inspectors look for when they listen to children
  • Everyday ways to hear children, not just surveys
  • Recording children’s views and what you did about them
  • Showing impact in children’s own words
  • Preparing children for inspection without coaching them

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1What changes

How inspection is shifting towards children’s experience

2Listening

Methods that work in a busy home

3Evidence

Records that show what children said and what changed

4Inspection

Ready every day, not the week before

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
