Understanding children in care

What it is really like to grow up in care.

A session led by people who grew up in care and went on to work in children’s services. Honest stories about what helped, what hurt and what professionals never saw, paired with what the evidence says to do about it.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

  • Length90 minutes to half a day. Also as a conference keynote
  • FormatWorkshop or keynote, live online or face to face
  • Who it is forWhole-service and whole-school audiences
  • Group sizeAny size, from a team meeting to a conference hall
  • IncludesReflection questions for teams to take back
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Whole-service and whole-school audiences
  • Conferences and development days
  • Corporate parenting boards and elected members
  • New starters in children’s services

What they learn

  • What children in care notice that adults miss
  • The moments that build trust, and the ones that break it
  • How systems and language can make children feel like a case
  • What one adult can change

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1The story

A real journey through care, from the inside

2The system

Where the system helped, and where it got in the way

3The evidence

What research tells us about the patterns in the story

4The ask

What each person in the room can do differently on Monday

Ways to book it

Workshop or keynote

A shorter, high-energy version for conferences, staff briefings and board sessions.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
