Stockport Council: training foster carers and social workers to write and read about children differently

In 2016 we designed and ran training for Stockport’s fostering service on the referrals and profiles that arrive before a child does: how they are written, how they are read, and what they mean for the child.

Client
Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council, Fostering and Family Placement

The starting point

When a child needs a foster home, a referral and a profile often arrive first. Foster carers decide on what is written, and placements can be refused or break down over it. Stockport wanted foster carers and social workers to understand the referral process and its impact on children, to share a language for these records, and to take an authentic, child-focused approach when recording, reporting and receiving information.

What we heard

The training was co-created with Stockport’s fostering service. Together we set out what both groups needed: how profiles are shared, what triggers a refusal or a breakdown, and the feelings behind the behaviours recorded in a child’s file.

What we did

  1. DesignMapped to standardsReferral training designed with Stockport and mapped to five Fostering National Minimum Standards
  2. Social workersOne-day courseCreating and contextualising child-focused profiles
  3. Foster carersOne-day courseMaking sense of referrals and profiles
  4. ToolkitRead beyond the formA toolkit for reading beyond referral and placement request forms

How we worked

Both sides of the form

The people who write referrals and the people who read them were trained on the same ideas.

Feelings behind behaviour

Sessions looked at what a recorded behaviour might mean for the child, not just what it says.

Standards built in

The content was mapped to the Fostering National Minimum Standards.

Luke designed the course with his own experience of care and his professional work with fostering services in mind, and co-delivered it with trainer Karylle Phillips.

What changed

The training ran in 2016 for foster carers, social workers and managers. It later became a general referral masterclass, which we went on to run beyond Stockport.
