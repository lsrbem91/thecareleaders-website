Words that matter

The words we use become their story.

Placement, contact, LAC, absconding. The language of children’s services can make children feel like a case. This course looks at the words we use about children, and the alternatives that help them feel like children.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Whole teams and whole services
  • Schools and virtual schools
  • Leaders refreshing policies and templates
  • Corporate parenting boards

What they learn

  • How labels and jargon affect children’s sense of themselves
  • The words children and young people have asked us to change
  • Alternatives that keep meaning and lose the stigma
  • How to change a service’s language without a lecture

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1Hear it

Words through the ears of a child in care

2Swap it

Common terms and better alternatives

3Write it

Records, letters and reports that lift

4Spread it

Making the change across your service

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

On demand

Self-paced on The Care Leaders Online, with a CPD certificate, for individuals or a whole team.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
