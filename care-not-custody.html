Relationships and NVR

A broken window is not a crime scene.

Children in care are far more likely to be criminalised than other children, often for things that would be handled at home in any other family. This course helps children’s homes, police and youth justice teams respond to incidents in ways that keep children safe and out of the justice system.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about this courseSee all training

At a glance

  • LengthHalf day. Joint sessions for homes and police work best
  • FormatFace to face or live online
  • Who it is forChildren’s home managers and staff
  • Group sizeUp to 30, ideally homes and police together
  • IncludesAn incident response guide and a joint protocol checklist
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Children’s home managers and staff
  • Police officers and neighbourhood teams
  • Youth justice and youth offending teams
  • Leaders reviewing their local protocol on criminalisation

What they learn

  • Why children in care end up in the justice system, and what it costs them
  • What would a good parent do? Responding to incidents without calling the police by default
  • De-escalation and restorative responses that hold children to account
  • Working with police when they are involved
  • Using the national and local protocols on reducing criminalisation

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1The pattern

How children in care get criminalised, through one young person’s story

2The moment

Incidents in the home: what happens next, and who decides

3The alternative

De-escalation, restorative practice and NVR in action

4The protocol

Making your local agreement work, together

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
