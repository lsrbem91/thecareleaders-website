Leaving care, work and adulthood

A first job can change everything.

Trauma-informed training for managers and supervisors who employ care leavers. Why the first months matter so much, how to support without lowering the bar, and what to do when life outside work gets in the way.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

Ask about this courseSee all training

At a glance

  • LengthHalf day. On demand in five short modules
  • FormatFace to face, live online or on demand
  • Who it is forLine managers and supervisors of care leavers
  • Group sizeUp to 30 managers
  • IncludesA manager’s guide and induction checklist
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Line managers and supervisors of care leavers
  • HR and apprenticeship teams
  • Council departments employing care leavers
  • Mentors and buddies in the workplace

What they learn

  • What care leavers often bring to a first job, good and hard
  • Inductions that build belonging from day one
  • Feedback and boundaries that do not trigger a walk-out
  • What to do when housing, money or family crises hit
  • Keeping people in work through the wobbles

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

1Understanding

Leaving care, trauma and work

2Starting well

Induction and the first 12 weeks

3Staying well

Supervision, feedback and wobbles

4Growing

Progression and long-term success

Ways to book it

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

On demand

Self-paced on The Care Leaders Online, with a CPD certificate, for individuals or a whole team.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
