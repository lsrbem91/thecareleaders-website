Course: Tools for Schools

Read the behaviour, then reach for the right tool.

A practical twilight session for school staff on reading the messages in children’s behaviour and responding with the right tools. One child’s story, the theory underneath it, and tools staff use the next day. Also available as a keynote.

Lived experience of being a child in care at school, and professional experience of training the staff who support those children.

Get the course brochureTalk to us

At a glance

  • LengthA three-hour twilight session, or a 90-minute keynote
  • FormatFace to face in your school, or at your conference
  • Who it is forTeachers, teaching assistants, pastoral staff and school leaders
  • Group sizeA whole staff team for the twilight. Any size as a keynote
  • IncludesThree tools to use the next day, with the handouts to keep
  • Built onStory, theory and action

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Teachers and teaching assistants
  • Pastoral and safeguarding staff
  • Designated teachers for children in care
  • Senior leaders setting the behaviour policy

What they learn

  • How to read behaviour as communication and respond in the moment
  • What attachment and trauma look like in school
  • How language and stigma shape a child’s school life
  • Where school and social care clash, and what to do about it
  • Three practical tools to use the next day

The four parts

Three hours in four parts, each built on one child’s story. Choose a part to see what it covers.

Part 1

The messages in behaviour

Behaviour as communication, through one child’s story and what the adults around him missed.

Covers

  • Behaviour as communication
  • What sits behind the behaviour you see
  • The window of tolerance
Part 2

Attachment and trauma

Attachment and trauma, and what they look like in a classroom, a corridor and a detention.

Covers

  • Attachment in the classroom
  • Trauma and the school day
  • Key adults
Part 3

Language and stigma

How language in staffrooms, meetings and reports shapes how a child is seen and treated.

Covers

  • Language that cares
  • Stigma and low expectations
  • Changing the conversation
Part 4

Where school and social care clash

Why the two systems frustrate each other, who the designated teacher really is, and practical tools to use tomorrow.

Covers

  • Where school and social care meet
  • The designated teacher role
  • Three tools to take into school

How it is delivered

Choose the format that fits your staff and your diary.

1

A twilight session

Three hours after school for a whole staff team, in your school.

2

A development day

The twilight session expanded with practice time, as part of an INSET day.

3

A keynote

Up to 90 minutes at your conference, for virtual schools and multi-academy trusts.

What you get, and what you do

What you get

  • Three tools to use the next day, with handouts
  • The slides to keep
  • A one-page summary for staff who could not attend
  • CPD certificates for participants

What you and your staff do

  • Release the staff team for three hours after school
  • Bring real, anonymised examples from your school
  • Try one tool in the week after the session
  • Tell us what happened, so the next session builds on it

What changes

By the end of the course.

  • Staff read behaviour as communication, not defiance
  • Fewer escalations and sanctions for children in care
  • Language about children in care changes in the staffroom and the records
  • School staff understand the social care side, and know who to call

Questions people ask

How long is it?

Three hours as a twilight session. Up to 90 minutes as a keynote.

Is it just for designated teachers?

No. It is for the whole staff team. Designated teachers who want a year-long programme should look at the Designated Leaders Programme.

Can you run it for a trust or a virtual school?

Yes, as a keynote at a conference or as twilight sessions across schools.

Is it CPD certified?

Participants receive a CPD certificate from us.
