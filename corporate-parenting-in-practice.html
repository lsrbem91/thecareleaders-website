Leaving care and work

Would it be good enough for my child?

For councillors, senior leaders and partners who share corporate parenting duties. What being a good corporate parent looks like day to day, how to hear from the children you are responsible for, and how to hold the whole council to account for them.

Length
Two hours to half a day. Also for board development sessions

The problem it solves

Corporate parenting boards often see dashboards rather than children. Members sign off reports without a clear sense of what good looks like to a child in care, and departments outside children’s services rarely see themselves as parents. This course gives the board better questions and a direct line to children’s voices.

Helps withCorporate parenting and leadershipGetting ready for inspection

What changes

  • Ask questions at board that get past the dashboard to the child.
  • Test decisions against one question: would it be good enough for my child?
  • Expect every council department to say what it offers care-experienced people.
  • Hear directly from children in care and care leavers, not only through reports.
  • Their views reach the board, and they hear what was done about them.
  • Council services beyond social care start offering what a parent would.
  • Decisions about their lives are tested against what a good parent would accept.
  • The corporate parenting principles, and what they mean in practice
  • What children in care and care leavers say good looks like

Session by session

  • Story
  • Theory
  • Action

Every part opens with a real story, adds the evidence behind it and ends with something people use the next day. Open a part to see what happens.

1The duty

What corporate parenting asks of you. We work through the corporate parenting principles and what they mean in practice for members, leaders and partners.

You leave with A short summary of the principles, turned into actions for your board.

2The children

What good looks like to children in care and care leavers, told through real stories, and the question every decision should pass: would it be good enough for my child?

You leave with A clear picture of what good looks like to the children themselves.

3The council

Every department as a parent: housing, jobs, leisure and more. We look at what each service could offer care-experienced people, and who needs asking.

You leave with A map of which departments could offer what, ready to follow up.

4The board

Questions, data and voice that change things. We practise questions that get past the dashboard and plan how the board will hear from children directly.

You leave with The corporate parenting self-check for your board, with agreed next steps.

Questions commissioners ask

Is this suitable for newly elected councillors?

Yes. New councillors are one of the groups it is written for. We start from what the duty asks of you and keep it practical, so people with no background in children’s services can use it at their next board.

Can children in care or care leavers take part?

Yes, and we encourage it. Hearing directly from the children you are responsible for is part of the course. We will plan with you how to involve them safely and fairly.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
