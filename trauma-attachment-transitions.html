Understanding care

Every move costs a child something.

What trauma and broken attachments do to children, why moves between homes and schools hit so hard, and what adults can do to make each transition less of a loss.

The problem it solves

Moves between homes and schools are where placements wobble, attendance drops and behaviour spikes. Too often a move is planned around the system, not the child, and the adults involved miss the old losses it brings back.

Helps withTrauma-informed practiceStability and matching

What changes

  • Plan moves with the child, asking what would help them feel safe
  • Read behaviour around a move as a response to loss
  • Pass on the small details that help a child settle somewhere new
  • Use the same transition planning tool for every move the team supports
  • Being asked about a move, not just told about it
  • Adults who expect endings to be hard and stay patient
  • Familiar things and routines that move with them
  • A new place that already knows something about them
  • What developmental trauma is, and what it is not
  • How attachment shapes trust, learning and behaviour
  • Why transitions trigger old losses
  • How to plan a move with the child, not around them
  • Small things that make a new place feel safe
  • Social workers and personal advisers
  • School staff supporting children through moves
  • Residential and fostering teams
  • Health and early help staff

Questions commissioners ask

Is it for schools or social care?

Both. A move hits a child at home and at school at the same time, so the course works with social workers, school staff and carers in the same session, planning from the same tool.

Does it cover school moves as well as placement moves?

Yes. We look at moves between homes and between schools, endings with key adults and new starts. The planning tool works for any of them.

Can you shape it around our service?

Yes. We tailor the examples, the length and the mix of people in the room to your service, your procedures and the children you work with.

How do you price it?

We quote per cohort, based on how many people, the format and how much tailoring you want. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.

Who delivers it?

People with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services. Every course is designed by Luke Rodgers BEM and follows the same Story, Theory, Action approach.
