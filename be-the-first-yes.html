Leaving care, work and adulthood

Be the employer who says yes first.

A workshop for employers who want to open doors for care-experienced people: work experience, apprenticeships and first jobs. It covers what holds care leavers back from applying, and the small changes that make an organisation the one that says yes.

Designed and delivered by people with lived experience of care and professional experience of children’s services, so the story is real and the practice is sound.

  • LengthTwo hours. Also as a conference or business breakfast session
  • FormatWorkshop or keynote, face to face or live online
  • Who it is forBusinesses and public sector employers

  • IncludesThe free employer’s guide to care-experienced talent
  • Built onStory, theory and action: a real story, the evidence behind it and tools to use the next day

Who it is for, and what they learn

Who it is for

  • Businesses and public sector employers
  • HR, recruitment and early careers teams
  • Chambers of commerce and employer networks
  • Council economic development teams

What they learn

  • What care-experienced people bring to an organisation
  • Barriers in the usual recruitment process
  • Simple changes to adverts, interviews and offers
  • Support that helps new starters stay
  • Joining a council’s local offer for care leavers

How the course runs

The same shape whether we deliver it in your building, live online or as a conference session. We tailor the examples to your service.

Ways to book it

Workshop or keynote

A shorter, high-energy version for conferences, staff briefings and board sessions.

Face to face

We come to you. Development days, twilights and team days, in your building or a venue you choose.

Live online

Live sessions online with a facilitator, so teams across a large area can join without travelling.

Every course can be shaped around your team: the length, the examples and the mix of people in the room. Tell us what you need and we will build it with you.
