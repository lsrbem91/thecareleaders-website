Consultancy

Practice approaches

When a child’s behaviour escalates, the adults around them often feel they have two options: back off or take control. Non-violent resistance gives them a third, built on staying present, calming things down and repairing the relationship. We train and embed NVR wherever adults care for children: in children’s homes, in foster families, and in early help and family support.

Is this for you?

Services usually come to us when:

Incidents and restraints are rising in your children’s homes.

Foster placements are breaking down because carers feel they’ve run out of things to try.

Your early help or family workers support parents facing aggressive or controlling behaviour at home, and need an approach they can teach.

Staff have been on training, but practice hasn’t changed.

What we do

1NVR in residential care

2NVR for foster carers

Helping carers stay calm, keep their presence and build support around the child, so placements hold.

3NVR for early help and family workers

NVR Essentials, a one-day taster. Councils that find it helpful commission the full practice framework.

Questions commissioners ask

Is NVR only for residential care?

No. NVR began as an approach for parents, and it’s now used with foster and kinship carers, in children’s homes and in family support services.

Is there evidence it works?

NVR was developed by Haim Omer for parents of children with violent and controlling behaviour. A 2025 review of studies covering 424 parents and carers found it may reduce parental stress, escalation and helplessness, and increase the support parents have. The evidence is still growing, so we build in ways to track change in your service.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the work. We agree a price in the brief before anything starts, so there are no surprises. Contact us for a price.

How long does it take?

Most work runs for three to 12 months. We set the timeline with you in the brief.

Who do you involve?

Your leaders and staff, your partners, and the children, young people and families who use the service. We agree who’s involved, and how, in the brief.

How do we buy from you?

Smaller pieces are usually a direct award or a quote. Larger pieces may go to tender, and we’re happy to talk before you write the specification.
